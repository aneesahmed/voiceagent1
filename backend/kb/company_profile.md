# Company Profile -- Meridian ERP Inc.

**Founded:** 2018
**Headquarters:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada
**Data centers:** Toronto and Montreal (Canada-only hosting)
**Target customers:** Canadian small-to-medium businesses, 10-250 employees
**Industries served:** Retail & e-commerce, light manufacturing/assembly,
professional services, wholesale/distribution, hospitality

## What we sell

Meridian ERP is a cloud-based, multi-tenant ERP platform built specifically
for Canadian SMEs -- Canadian payroll/tax rules, PIPEDA-compliant data
residency, and pricing scaled for smaller teams than enterprise ERPs like
SAP or Dynamics 365 are built for.

## Modules

- **Accounting & Finance** -- GL, AP/AR, bank reconciliation, multi-entity
  consolidation (Growth+)
- **Sales & CRM** -- pipeline tracking, quotes, order management
- **Inventory & Warehouse** -- stock tracking, reorder points,
  multi-location (Growth+)
- **Purchasing & Procurement** -- purchase orders, vendor management (Growth+)
- **HR & Payroll** -- Canadian payroll compliance (CRA remittances, T4s),
  time tracking (Growth+)
- **Manufacturing** -- bill of materials, work orders, production
  scheduling (Enterprise only)
- **Point of Sale** -- in-store/counter sales, syncs with Inventory
  (Enterprise only)
- **Reporting & Analytics** -- dashboards, custom report builder (Growth+)

## Integrations

QuickBooks Online (one-time migration + ongoing bank feed sync), Shopify,
Stripe, Canada Post / UPS shipping rates, Microsoft 365, Zapier (Enterprise
also gets direct API access).

## Support

- Starter / Growth: Mon-Fri, 8am-8pm ET -- live chat, email, phone
- Enterprise: 24/7 phone + chat, dedicated account manager

## Mobile

iOS/Android companion app (all tiers) -- approvals, inventory lookups,
field sales access.

## Compliance & security

- PIPEDA-compliant; all customer data hosted in Canada only, never sold
  to third parties
- TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest
- Role-based access control
- Daily backups, retained 30 days
- 99.9% uptime SLA on Growth and Enterprise plans (credits issued if
  breached)
