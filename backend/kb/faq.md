# FAQ -- Meridian ERP Inc.

**Does it integrate with QuickBooks?**
Yes -- a one-time migration tool imports your existing QuickBooks Online
data, plus ongoing bank feed sync afterward.

**Can I customize the system?**
Starter is templated with no custom fields. Growth supports custom fields
and workflow rules. Enterprise supports full customization plus direct
API-level integration.

**How long does data migration take?**
Starter: 3-5 business days. Growth: 1-2 weeks. Enterprise: 4-8 weeks,
depending on data complexity and number of integrations.

**Is training included?**
Yes, on every plan. Starter includes 2 live onboarding sessions, Growth
includes 5, and Enterprise includes unlimited sessions during onboarding.
All plans also get access to a self-serve video library.

**What's the cancellation policy?**
Month-to-month plans can be cancelled anytime with 30 days' written
notice. Annual plans can also be cancelled, but the remaining prepaid
months aren't refunded beyond the 30-day money-back guarantee window.

**Is there a refund policy?**
Yes -- a 30-day money-back guarantee on your first payment if you're not
satisfied.

**What happens to our data if we cancel?**
You get a 90-day export window after cancellation to pull your data out,
after which it's permanently deleted from our systems.

**What are your support hours?**
Starter and Growth: Monday-Friday, 8am-8pm ET, via chat, email, or phone.
Enterprise: 24/7 phone and chat with a dedicated account manager.

**Do you support multiple locations or currencies?**
Multi-location inventory is available from the Growth plan up.
Multi-currency support is Enterprise-only.

**Is there a mobile app?**
Yes, on every plan -- iOS and Android, for approvals, inventory lookups,
and field sales access.

**How long is the contract?**
Month-to-month or annual (annual gets a 15% discount). No long-term
lock-in is required on any plan.

**Is our data secure and where is it hosted?**
All data is hosted in Canadian data centers only (Toronto and Montreal),
encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), and we're PIPEDA
compliant. We never sell customer data to third parties.

**Can I try it before committing?**
Starter and Growth offer a free 14-day trial, no credit card required.
Enterprise requires a scoping call and demo before a quote is issued --
there's no instant self-serve trial at that tier.
