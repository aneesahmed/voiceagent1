# Policies -- Meridian ERP Inc.

## Refund policy
30-day money-back guarantee on the first payment only, for any plan. No
refunds on subsequent payments or on remaining prepaid months of an
annual contract beyond that initial 30-day window.

## Cancellation policy
- Month-to-month: cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice.
- Annual: can be cancelled, but no refund for remaining prepaid months
  (outside the 30-day guarantee window).

## Data retention after cancellation
90-day export window after cancellation takes effect. Data is
permanently deleted from Meridian's systems after that window closes.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
99.9% uptime SLA applies to Growth and Enterprise plans. Service credits
are issued automatically if the SLA is breached in a given month. Starter
does not carry a formal uptime SLA.

## Privacy & data handling
- PIPEDA compliant.
- All customer data is stored exclusively in Canadian data centers
  (Toronto and Montreal) -- never hosted or replicated outside Canada.
- Customer data is never sold or shared with third parties.
- Role-based access control is enforced on all accounts.
- Daily backups are retained for 30 days.

## What the AI sales/support agent must NOT do
- Never invent a pricing figure, feature, or policy not present in this
  knowledge base -- if asked something outside this document set, say so
  honestly and offer to connect the customer with a human rather than
  guessing.
- Never make binding commitments on custom Enterprise pricing --
  Enterprise always requires a human-run scoping call and quote.
- Never promise a specific SLA credit amount, legal interpretation, or
  contract term beyond what's written here -- escalate those to a human.
