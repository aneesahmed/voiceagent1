# Pricing & Plans -- Meridian ERP Inc.

All prices in CAD, billed monthly unless annual billing is selected
(15% discount for annual prepay). A one-time implementation fee applies
to all plans and covers data migration, configuration, and training.

## Starter
- **Best for:** under 20 employees
- **Price:** $299/month, includes 5 user seats; $25/month per additional user
- **Modules:** Accounting & Finance, Sales & CRM, basic Inventory
- **Implementation fee:** $500 (one-time)
- **Data migration timeline:** 3-5 business days
- **Training included:** 2 live onboarding sessions + self-serve video library
- **Customization:** templated -- no custom fields/workflows

## Growth
- **Best for:** 20-100 employees
- **Price:** $699/month, includes 10 user seats; $35/month per additional user
- **Adds:** Purchasing & Procurement, HR & Payroll (Canadian compliance),
  Reporting & Analytics, multi-location Inventory, multi-entity accounting
  consolidation
- **Implementation fee:** $1,500 (one-time)
- **Data migration timeline:** 1-2 weeks
- **Training included:** 5 live onboarding sessions + self-serve video library
- **Customization:** custom fields and workflow rules supported

## Enterprise
- **Best for:** 100-250 employees
- **Price:** custom quote, unlimited user seats
- **Adds:** Manufacturing (BOM, work orders, production scheduling),
  Point of Sale, multi-currency, direct API access, dedicated account manager
- **Implementation fee:** custom quote, based on scope
- **Data migration timeline:** 4-8 weeks depending on complexity
- **Training included:** unlimited live sessions during onboarding
- **Customization:** full custom fields, workflows, and API-level integration
- **Requires:** a scoping call + demo before a quote can be issued -- not
  available for instant self-serve signup

## Trial

Starter and Growth plans include a free 14-day trial, no credit card
required. Enterprise does not offer a self-serve trial -- it requires a
demo and custom quote first.

## Contract terms

Month-to-month or annual (annual gets the 15% discount). No long-term
lock-in required on any plan.

## Payment methods

Credit card, pre-authorized debit, or invoice/EFT (Enterprise annual
contracts only).
